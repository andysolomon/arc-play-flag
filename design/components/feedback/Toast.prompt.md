Transient instructions: Toast floats centred under the header while the user is mid-gesture; Note is the same message as a yellow sticky in the sidebar.

```jsx
<Toast>Cover who? Tap a red player.</Toast>
<Note>Tap waypoints on the field · double-tap to finish</Note>
```

- One sentence, a question or an imperative. No icons, no close button — it disappears when the gesture ends.
