# ADR 008: the roster, a depth chart by position

Status: implemented.

## Why

A play names its players by position: C, QB, X, Y and Z on offense, LC, LB, R, RC and S on defense, the tags the wristbands, slides and binder pages count by. A coach knows those positions by the kids who play them, and wanted the field to say so: who X is, and who steps in at X when that kid is away. A tag is three letters wide, so a name can't go in it, and a name on a play would be stored in every play, every share link and every printout, where a child's name has no business going. So the roster is its own thing: a mode that lays the positions out on the field, with a depth chart per position that the whole library reads from.

## Rules

1. **A position is a tag.** The roster keys its slots by team and tag, exactly as a player's label is stored. Whoever wears X on any play is played by X's starter; two players wearing the same tag on one play share the slot (R5). A tag renamed on a play is a fresh position with nobody down for it (R1); the old slot keeps its names for whoever wears that tag next, and undoing the rename brings them back. A player with no tag has no slot (R6).
2. **Starter first, from the team.** Each slot is a list of the team's players, the starter at the top; **▲** moves a line up, **✕** takes it off the chart (they stay on the team), and a name opens that player's page. **+ Add a player** opens a chooser of everyone on the team not on this chart yet, each with where they play (a player left off this lineup for a day is still offered, being on the team, and a name typed two ways counts as there, R7), and a **New player** field: a name typed there joins the team and the chart in one write, and a name already on the team, however it is typed, just goes on the chart (R7). Up to eight lines (`MAX_DEPTH`), and once the chart is full **+ Add a player** is off, so neither way adds one. A blank line is nobody and is dropped (R2), and a slot with no lines left is dropped (R3), so an empty depth chart stores nothing.
3. **The starter's name goes under the tag on the field**, always in roster mode, and while drawing the play only once the coach turns **Show player names** on (rule 9), shortened to fit (R4): whole when it is twelve letters or fewer, else the first word, else cut with an ellipsis. The panel keeps the whole name. A caption is centred under its token, and pulled in at a sideline so the whole name stays on the field. It is drawn on screen only: not in any picture of a play (`data-export="skip"`) and not on paper (`print:hidden`), so printing the designer gives the play without a name. The roster's own picture and sheet draw the names themselves ([ADR 010](010-roster-exports.md)). A position nobody is down for shows no caption on a play; in roster mode it reads "Unassigned" (or "No tag"), so the gaps are plain.
4. **The roster is the team's, on this device.** It is written as it is typed, like the team's name, never undone, and goes into a device backup and comes back from one. It is never written into a play, a share link, a playbook file, a thumbnail or a play's printout: the names stay with the coach. Beside a device backup, they leave it only in the roster's own picture and sheet, saved from the roster panel when the coach asks ([ADR 010](010-roster-exports.md)).
5. **Lineups are versions of the roster.** The **Starting Lineup** is always there and can't be deleted. Any lineup can be **branched**: the branch starts as a copy of the lineup it came from (a branch of a branch copies the branch, V4), is active at once, is named "Lineup n", its place in the lineups menu, until the coach names it, and says what it was branched from. The field, every play and every caption follow the **active** lineup, and the pick outlasts a reload. Deleting a lineup asks once, in place (it is not undoable); the field falls back to the lineup it was branched from, else the starting lineup (V2), and its own branches forget it (V3). Up to twenty lineups.
6. **The team comes first.** A player joins the **team**, by name, at any time: PLAYERS is always in the panel, with or without a position tapped and before anyone is down for anything, and its **+ Add to team** takes a name and opens that player's page (T1). The team is one list, the same on every lineup, so a branch has the same players and only its depth charts differ. A name already on the team, however it is typed, opens the page already there (T2); a blank one adds nobody (T3). Up to fifteen players (`MAX_TEAM`, T8): once full, **+ Add to team** and a new name in a depth chart's chooser are off and the panel says why, until a player is removed; the list says how many of the fifteen are on it. A device whose depth charts already held more names than that before the team's list keeps every one of them, and is full until it is back under. A name typed on a depth chart before the team's list existed joins the team when the lineups are read (T6), so nothing already typed is lost.
7. **Every player has a page.** PLAYERS lists the whole team in alphabetical order, each with where they play on the active lineup ("X starter, LC 2nd", or "No position"), and a name opens that player's page: a field to rename them, every position they are down for on this lineup and where they sit at each ("X · Starter", "LC · 2nd"), each a way to its depth chart; both lists put the positions a player starts at first, then go down the depth charts, positions at the same depth in roster order; and PUT AT A POSITION, every position on the field they are not down for, a tap putting them on that chart as the starter when nobody is down for it, else next up (T5). A rename follows the player onto every depth chart on every lineup, and a name another player already has is refused with a word saying so (T7). **Remove from team** asks once, in place, and takes them off the team and off every depth chart on every lineup (T4, R8). Tapping the field leaves the page.
8. **Roster mode is the team's, not a play's.** It lays out the standard formation (`defaults()`: C, QB, X, Y and Z, LC, LB, R, RC and S at their home spots, or where the coach has stood them, rule 10), with the ball on the 15 (`ROSTER_LOS`, the line of scrimmage labelled "LOS 15") whatever the open play's spot, never the play that is open: none of its routes, retagged positions, moved spots, spot or side. Routes, arcs, man tags, the no-run stamp, waypoints and the ▶ button are off; the card frames just the formation, 23 yards from 8 behind the line up to their goal line (`ROSTER_DEPTH`, lib/play/geometry.ts), so the no-run band the safety stands in is whole with its label above him, closer in than a play's: never stretched down a tall pane (on a phone it sits centred in it) and never opened by **Deep field**, which is not offered there and stays the plays' choice; a position drags anywhere on that card, so a defender stands at most 13.8 yards deep, inside the goal line (a spot stored deeper still deepens the card to hold it); both teams are in it at full strength, every position tappable, and draggable or stepped by the arrow keys as a play's players are (rule 10); it has a selection of its own, Undo and Redo (buttons and keys) are off, since the play's history is the play's, and Play tools hides the play's own sections (PLAY, FIELD and SHOW), leaving ROSTER, TEAM and the looks. A tag the formation has no token for (FS, H) keeps its depth chart in reach (R9): every tag a chart is kept for on any lineup, the open play wears or a saved play wears is listed in the panel after its side's five, in tag order, never drawn on the field; its chart opens from the list or a player's page like any other and says it is off the field. Leaving roster mode brings the play back exactly as it was, history included.
   The roster is both sides of one team, so neither side is a shadow of the other there: the field shows **one side at a time**, the open play's side to start (or the side of the player picked in the palette), never faded. In the header a side badge takes the place of **Save** (there is no play to save), a button that goes **Offense → Defense → Both** and round again, and the panel's SHOW pills pick the same three. **Both** puts all ten positions on the field at once, none faded. A side taken off the field takes its picked position with it, and the panel lists only the positions on view, the tags off the field included (both sides offense first). A position on a player's page whose side is off the field brings that side on; PUT AT A POSITION still offers both sides, since a tap there only writes the chart.
   The panel has no way back of its own: the **Roster** tile in Play tools is the one way in and out.
9. **Names on a play are opt-in, and need a roster.** **Show player names**, under the Roster tile in Play tools, is off until the coach turns it on, and can't be turned on until some depth chart has a name on it ("Build a roster first"). Once on, a **Lineup** picker beside it says which lineup's starters the plays show; it is the same active lineup the roster panel picks. The choice is kept with the lineups, on the device and in a backup. The last name taken off every depth chart (off a chart, or off the team) turns it off with it (V6), so a play never says it shows names from an empty roster.
10. **The roster's spots are the team's.** In roster mode a position drags, and steps a yard at a time with the arrow keys, as a player on a play does: held to its own side of the line and inside the sidelines (F1), never on top of a teammate (kept 2 yards off them, `lib/play/spacing.ts`), a drop snapped to the half yard. A drag picks nothing; a tap still picks the position. Where it lands is kept with the lineups, the same on every lineup like the team (F7), written as it lands and never undone, and goes into a device backup and comes back from one. It is never written into a play, its history, a share link, a playbook file or a picture, and the play open behind roster mode never moves (F6). A position put back on its home spot keeps nothing (F2), so a formation nobody moved stores nothing. **Reset spots** puts the sides on view back at their home spots in one write, asking once, in place, since it is not undoable (F8); it is off while nothing on view has moved. The panel's list of positions follows the field, left to right.
11. **The Secondary Lineup is made from the depth charts.** It is always there, straight after the Starting Lineup in the lineups menu and the **Lineup** picker, and is never stored: it is made from the Starting Lineup's depth charts on every read, so it follows every change there, a line moved, a player renamed or removed (S3). At each position the next player up starts, the chart moved up a line with the starter at its foot; a position with nobody behind its starter keeps that starter (S1). Among the standard formation's ten positions nobody starts twice on one side (S2): the positions take their starters a line down the charts at a time, one with fewer names first (so a position with one choice gets it), then in roster order, and one left with nobody free keeps its starter. Offense and defense are apart, so a player may start on both, and a tag the formation lacks (FS, H) takes its next player up as it is, since it stands in for one of the ten on a play. It is shown, never changed (S4): its name is read only, its menu has no Delete, its depth charts have no **▲**, **✕** or **+ Add a player**, a player's page on it has no PUT AT A POSITION, and it says it is made from the Starting Lineup's depth charts; a chart's **Change X on Starting Lineup** picks the Starting Lineup with the same position open. **Branch lineup** copies it as made at that moment into a lineup of the coach's own, branched from the Secondary Lineup and no longer following the Starting Lineup (S5), and a deleted branch of it falls back to it. Only the pick and a branch's `from` name it in storage; a stored lineup claiming its id is dropped, and it does not count toward the twenty (S6).

## Data model

`ffpd.lineups.v1` holds `{ active, lineups, players, names?, spots? }`: `players` the team, each name as first written, `names` true when plays show the starters (stored only then), `spots` the positions moved in roster mode, each `{ team, tag, x, y }` in yards in the order they first moved (stored only once one has), and each lineup `{ id, name, from?, slots }`, `from` the id of the lineup it was branched from, and `slots` a roster, a list of `{ team, tag, depth }`: `tag` up to three letters as a label is stored, `depth` the names, starter first, each trimmed, up to forty letters with control characters out. `normalizeRoster` (lib/play/storage.ts) drops blank lines, then a slot with no tag or no lines, ignores a second slot for the same tag on the same team, and caps the list at thirty slots and each slot at eight lines; `normalizeLineups` puts the starting lineup back (empty, at the front) when it is missing, ignores a second lineup with an id already seen, drops a stored lineup with the Secondary Lineup's id (`second`, made and never kept) while letting `active` and a `from` name it, drops a `from` that names no lineup, keeps `players` as trimmed strings, each player once (R7) and at most fifteen (`MAX_TEAM`), then adds every name on a depth chart that is not on it yet (T6), makes the starting lineup active when the active id names none, drops `names` when no depth chart has a name (V6), keeps a spot only for a tag the standard formation has a token for (F3) and with two numbers for it (F5), the first for each position (F4), held to its side of the line and inside the sidelines (F1) and none back home (F2), and caps the set at twenty; `readLineups` answers the starting lineup alone, empty, for a device that has none. The library store (lib/play/library.ts) serves them with `getLineups` / `setLineups` beside the team, so every screen re-renders on a write and nothing is reported kept until it reads back. The pure helpers are in `lib/play/roster.ts` (`slotOf`, `depthOf`, `starterOf`, `withDepth`, `shortName`, `starters`, `depthWord`, `playerNames`, `standings`, `sameName`, `renamedIn`, `withoutName`, `offFieldPositions`) and `lib/play/lineup.ts` (`nextUp`, `secondLineup`, `allLineups`, `activeLineup`, `withActive`, `withSlots`, `renamed`, `branched`, `without`, `fromName`, `teamNames`, `onTeam`, `teamFull`, `withPlayer`, `withoutPlayer`, `renamedPlayer`, `hasNames`, `showsNames`, `withNames`, `placedSpot`, `withSpot`, `withoutSpots`, `hasSpots`, `formationOf`, `rosterPositions`), each with its ways of going wrong written down first (R1–R9, V1–V6, T1–T8, F1–F8, S1–S6).

The device backup (`lib/export/backup.ts`) carries `lineups`; a backup from before this change has none and restores the empty starting lineup, so version 1 stands. Stored lineups and backups from before the team's list have no `players`: it is made from the names on their depth charts (T6). A playbook file does not carry them.

## In the designer

- **Roster** is a tile in Play tools, under its own ROSTER heading before TEAM, with **Show player names** and, while it is on, the **Lineup** picker under it. The tile toggles the mode; the header's right toggle reads **Roster** instead of **Routes** and opens the roster panel in the same sidebar; the side badge in place of **Save** reads **Offense**, **Defense** or **Both** and is a button; **Clear** waits for the play. The field's title reads "Roster" with the team's name under it.
- The roster panel opens on the LINEUP, one row: the active lineup's name to edit, with every lineup dropped down from a chevron inside it (as the play name's list is), the active one checked, a long name wrapping whole rather than cut off, and, off the starting lineup, **Delete “…”** at the foot of the menu, and beside it a fork button, **Branch lineup**. Under the row, "Branched from …" when it was ("Made from Starting Lineup's depth charts: …" on the Secondary Lineup, whose name field is read only), and once Delete is picked, "Delete …?" with **Delete** and **Keep** (focus on Keep). The field's title reads "Roster" with the active lineup's name under it.
- Then the position tapped: its token, wearing the standard tag (tags are a play's to rename, not the roster's), and heading "Who plays X", the DEPTH CHART with a numbered line per name, the starter's number on the highlighter, and a note saying where the names go and don't. **+ Add a player** under the chart becomes the chooser, the team's players as buttons above a **New player** field with **Add**, and **Cancel** (Escape cancels and keeps the position selected). Under it, PLAYERS, the whole team with where they play on this lineup, each a button to that player's page, and **+ Add to team**, which becomes a name field with **Add** and **Cancel** (Enter adds, Escape cancels); then SHOW, the Offense / Defense / Both pills; then SPOTS, **Reset spots**, which becomes "Put the offense back at its home spots?" (or "both sides … their home spots") with **Reset** and **Keep** (focus on Keep; Keep gives it back to Reset spots, Reset to the SHOW pills), and a note that a position drags or steps and stays put on every lineup; then EXPORT, the roster's picture and sheet ([ADR 010](010-roster-exports.md)); then every position on view (offense first when both are), left to right as the wristbands count them and then the tags off the field, each a button that selects that player and says who starts and how deep ("X: Devon, 2 deep").
- With nothing tapped, the panel says to tap a position to set who plays it, or drag it to move it.
- Entering roster mode leaves whatever was being drawn and opens the panel; a player picked in the palette opens the depth chart of the standard position wearing the same tag, when there is one, with that player's side on view (else the play's own).
- A screen reader hears "Played by Devon" on a token that has a starter.
