# ADR 008: the roster, a depth chart by position

Status: implemented.

## Why

A play names its players by position: C, QB, X, Y and Z on offense, LC, LB, R, RC and S on defense, the tags the wristbands, slides and binder pages count by. A coach knows those positions by the kids who play them, and wanted the field to say so: who X is, and who steps in at X when that kid is away. A tag is three letters wide, so a name can't go in it, and a name on a play would be stored in every play, every share link and every printout, where a child's name has no business going. So the roster is its own thing: a mode that lays the positions out on the field, with a depth chart per position that the whole library reads from.

## Rules

1. **A position is a tag.** The roster keys its slots by team and tag, exactly as a player's label is stored. Whoever wears X on any play is played by X's starter; two players wearing the same tag on one play share the slot (R5). A tag renamed on a play is a fresh position with nobody down for it (R1); the old slot keeps its names for whoever wears that tag next, and undoing the rename brings them back. A player with no tag has no slot (R6).
2. **Starter first, from the team.** Each slot is a list of the team's players, the starter at the top; **▲** moves a line up, **✕** takes it off the chart (they stay on the team), and a name opens that player's page. **+ Add a player** opens a chooser of everyone on the team not on this chart yet, each with where they play (a player left off this lineup for a day is still offered, being on the team, and a name typed two ways counts as there, R7), and a **New player** field: a name typed there joins the team and the chart in one write, and a name already on the team, however it is typed, just goes on the chart (R7). Up to eight lines (`MAX_DEPTH`), and once the chart is full **+ Add a player** is off, so neither way adds one. A blank line is nobody and is dropped (R2), and a slot with no lines left is dropped (R3), so an empty depth chart stores nothing.
3. **The starter's name goes under the tag on the field**, always in roster mode, and while drawing the play only once the coach turns **Show player names** on (rule 9), shortened to fit (R4): whole when it is twelve letters or fewer, else the first word, else cut with an ellipsis. The panel keeps the whole name. A caption is centred under its token, and pulled in at a sideline so the whole name stays on the field. It is drawn on screen only: not in any picture (`data-export="skip"`) and not on paper (`print:hidden`), so printing the designer gives the play without a name. A position nobody is down for shows no caption on a play; in roster mode it reads "Unassigned" (or "No tag"), so the gaps are plain.
4. **The roster is the team's, on this device.** It is written as it is typed, like the team's name, never undone, and goes into a device backup and comes back from one. It is never written into a play, a share link, a playbook file, a thumbnail or a printout: the names stay with the coach.
5. **Lineups are versions of the roster.** The **Starting Lineup** is always there and can't be deleted. Any lineup can be **branched**: the branch starts as a copy of the lineup it came from (a branch of a branch copies the branch, V4), is active at once, is named "Lineup n" until the coach names it, and says what it was branched from. The field, every play and every caption follow the **active** lineup, and the pick outlasts a reload. Deleting a lineup asks once, in place (it is not undoable); the field falls back to the lineup it was branched from, else the starting lineup (V2), and its own branches forget it (V3). Up to twenty lineups.
6. **The team comes first.** A player joins the **team**, by name, at any time: PLAYERS is always in the panel, with or without a position tapped and before anyone is down for anything, and its **+ Add to team** takes a name and opens that player's page (T1). The team is one list, the same on every lineup, so a branch has the same players and only its depth charts differ. A name already on the team, however it is typed, opens the page already there (T2); a blank one adds nobody (T3). Up to fifteen players (`MAX_TEAM`, T8): once full, **+ Add to team** and a new name in a depth chart's chooser are off and the panel says why, until a player is removed; the list says how many of the fifteen are on it. A device whose depth charts already held more names than that before the team's list keeps every one of them, and is full until it is back under. A name typed on a depth chart before the team's list existed joins the team when the lineups are read (T6), so nothing already typed is lost.
7. **Every player has a page.** PLAYERS lists the whole team in alphabetical order, each with where they play on the active lineup ("X starter, LC 2nd", or "No position"), and a name opens that player's page: a field to rename them, every position they are down for on this lineup and where they sit at each ("X · Starter", "LC · 2nd"), each a way to its depth chart, and PUT AT A POSITION, every position on the field they are not down for, a tap putting them on that chart as the starter when nobody is down for it, else next up (T5). A rename follows the player onto every depth chart on every lineup, and a name another player already has is refused with a word saying so (T7). **Remove from team** asks once, in place, and takes them off the team and off every depth chart on every lineup (T4, R8). Tapping the field leaves the page.
8. **Roster mode is the formation alone.** Routes, arcs, man tags, the no-run stamp, waypoints and the ▶ button are off; dragging still lines players up, and that is the play's edit, undoable as ever. Leaving roster mode brings the play back as it was.
   The roster is both sides of one team, so neither side is a shadow of the other there: the field shows **one side at a time**, the play's own side to start (or the side of the player selected when roster mode opens), never faded. The header's side badge becomes a button that goes **Offense → Defense → Both** and round again, and the panel's SHOW pills pick the same three. **Both** puts every position on the field at once, none faded. A side taken off the field takes its selected position with it, and the panel lists only the positions on view (both, the play's side first). A position on a player's page whose side is off the field brings that side on.
   The panel has no way back of its own: the **Roster** tile in Play tools is the one way in and out.
9. **Names on a play are opt-in, and need a roster.** **Show player names**, under the Roster tile in Play tools, is off until the coach turns it on, and can't be turned on until some depth chart has a name on it ("Build a roster first"). Once on, a **Lineup** picker beside it says which lineup's starters the plays show; it is the same active lineup the roster panel picks. The choice is kept with the lineups, on the device and in a backup. The last name taken off every depth chart (off a chart, or off the team) turns it off with it (V6), so a play never says it shows names from an empty roster.

## Data model

`ffpd.lineups.v1` holds `{ active, lineups, players, names? }`: `players` the team, each name as first written, `names` true when plays show the starters (stored only then), and each lineup `{ id, name, from?, slots }`, `from` the id of the lineup it was branched from, and `slots` a roster, a list of `{ team, tag, depth }`: `tag` up to three letters as a label is stored, `depth` the names, starter first, each trimmed, up to forty letters with control characters out. `normalizeRoster` (lib/play/storage.ts) drops blank lines, then a slot with no tag or no lines, ignores a second slot for the same tag on the same team, and caps the list at thirty slots and each slot at eight lines; `normalizeLineups` puts the starting lineup back (empty, at the front) when it is missing, ignores a second lineup with an id already seen, drops a `from` that names no lineup, keeps `players` as trimmed strings, each player once (R7) and at most fifteen (`MAX_TEAM`), then adds every name on a depth chart that is not on it yet (T6), makes the starting lineup active when the active id names none, drops `names` when no depth chart has a name (V6), and caps the set at twenty; `readLineups` answers the starting lineup alone, empty, for a device that has none. The library store (lib/play/library.ts) serves them with `getLineups` / `setLineups` beside the team, so every screen re-renders on a write and nothing is reported kept until it reads back. The pure helpers are in `lib/play/roster.ts` (`slotOf`, `depthOf`, `starterOf`, `withDepth`, `shortName`, `starters`, `depthWord`, `playerNames`, `standings`, `sameName`, `renamedIn`, `withoutName`) and `lib/play/lineup.ts` (`activeLineup`, `withActive`, `withSlots`, `renamed`, `branched`, `without`, `fromName`, `teamNames`, `onTeam`, `teamFull`, `withPlayer`, `withoutPlayer`, `renamedPlayer`, `hasNames`, `showsNames`, `withNames`), each with its ways of going wrong written down first (R1–R8, V1–V6, T1–T8).

The device backup (`lib/export/backup.ts`) carries `lineups`; a backup from before this change has none and restores the empty starting lineup, so version 1 stands. Stored lineups and backups from before the team's list have no `players`: it is made from the names on their depth charts (T6). A playbook file does not carry them.

## In the designer

- **Roster** is a tile in Play tools, under its own ROSTER heading before TEAM, with **Show player names** and, while it is on, the **Lineup** picker under it. The tile toggles the mode; the header's right toggle reads **Roster** instead of **Routes** and opens the roster panel in the same sidebar; the side badge reads **Offense**, **Defense** or **Both** and is a button; **Clear** waits for the play. The field's title reads "Roster" with the team's name under it.
- The roster panel opens on the LINEUP: a picker of every lineup, the active one's name to edit, "Branched from …" when it was, **Branch lineup** and, off the starting lineup, **Delete lineup**, which turns into "Delete …?" with **Delete** and **Keep**. The field's title reads "Roster" with the active lineup's name under it.
- Then the position tapped: its tag field (the same one the route palette has) and heading "Who plays X", the DEPTH CHART with a numbered line per name, the starter's number on the highlighter, and a note saying where the names go and don't. **+ Add a player** under the chart becomes the chooser, the team's players as buttons above a **New player** field with **Add**, and **Cancel** (Escape cancels and keeps the position selected). Under it, PLAYERS, the whole team with where they play on this lineup, each a button to that player's page, and **+ Add to team**, which becomes a name field with **Add** and **Cancel** (Enter adds, Escape cancels); then SHOW, the Offense / Defense / Both pills; then every position on view (both sides with the play's side first when both are), left to right as the wristbands count them, each a button that selects that player and says who starts and how deep ("X: Devon, 2 deep").
- Entering roster mode leaves whatever was being drawn, keeps the selection (so a player picked in the palette is the position whose chart opens) and opens the panel.
- A screen reader hears "Played by Devon" on a token that has a starter.
